about()

I'm a full stack engineer and graphic designer based in Morocco. I build web applications with the MERN stack and Next.js, and I design the interfaces, branding and motion around them in Figma, Illustrator, Photoshop, After Effects and XD.

I also teach first-year TDI (Technicien Développement Informatique) students at an OFPPT vocational training center, where I prepare the curriculum and the exams. Explaining code to beginners keeps my own fundamentals sharp.

I like projects where the code and the design are made by the same person, so nothing gets lost between the mockup and the browser.
